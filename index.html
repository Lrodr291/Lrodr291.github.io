Lesly Rodriguez

Teacher📚

My name is Lesly Rodriguez Cedillos, and I am a dedicated, community‑focused student committed to education, youth development, and public service. As a bilingual individual from Mecca, California, I bring a strong foundation in mentorship, communication, and hands‑on support for children and families. I am currently pursuing my degree in Education, Society, and Human Development at the University of California, Riverside, where I continue to strengthen my skills in leadership, collaboration, and student support. My passion for helping others learn and feel supported guides both my academic goals and my work in the community.

Throughout my educational journey, I have gained extensive experience working with children and supporting school environments. I have volunteered more than 32 hours at Abraham Lincoln Elementary School and Mecca Elementary School, where I assisted teachers, supervised students, and helped maintain safe and welcoming learning spaces. In these roles, I guided classroom activities, helped with school events, and provided bilingual assistance to families. These experiences taught me how to adapt quickly, communicate clearly, and work effectively with diverse groups of students and staff.

My long‑term goal is to work in education and contribute to youth development. I am motivated by a desire to uplift my community, expand access to educational support, and serve as a role model for young people navigating their own academic journeys. Through my coursework, volunteer work, and leadership experiences, I continue to grow into someone who is committed to service, learning, and making a meaningful impact.
